### Fact check (every claim is backed by the project's data)
- NISAR, NASA × ISRO, L-band, 12-day repeat, "day or night, through cloud": mission facts.
- "More than two thousand images in three days": 2,356 NISAR granules in the 3-day CMR snapshot.
- "Nearly seven gigabytes": the real GCOV file on screen is 6.7 GB (6,685,720,576 bytes).
- "Nearly two thousand km² … water-like": 1,945 km², derived estimate, 18 Jun → 24 Jul.
- Kumamoto: M6.8, 28 Jul 2026 (USGS us6000tgb9); 5 GUNW pairs, only 21 Jul → 14 Aug spans it.
  "Each ring traces the ground moving toward or away from the satellite" = each colour cycle is a contour of line-of-sight motion.
- The farmer, rescue team and student are illustrative ("Picture…"), not real people.
- Roadmap and needs are plans ("we'll", "we need"). Confirm they match what the team wants to say.

### Recording tips (if a team member reads it)
Use a phone in a small room with soft furnishings (a closet with clothes works
well), 15–20 cm from the mouth, slightly off-axis. Record each section as a
separate take. Leave 2 seconds of silence at the start of each take for noise
removal. Smile on the hook and the ending; it shows in the voice. Send WAV or
M4A files and I'll clean, time and mix them under the score.
